## 2026-10-26 · Free agents are a setting

`freeAgents` (default on) controls whether free agents are offered: the "No repository" choice in the new session dialog, the palette command, the first-run button, and the "+" on the Free agents group. Turning it off only hides the entry points; free agents that already exist keep working and stay visible. With no repositories added, the dialog still offers the choice so it is never empty.
