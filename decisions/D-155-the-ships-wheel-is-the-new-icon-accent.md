## D-155 · 2026-10-26 · The ship's wheel is the new icon: accent wheel on a dark circle, drawn from one set of sources
The wheel supplied as `ship-wheel.svg` (a rim, hub and eight spoked handles) replaces the outline wheel everywhere. The app icon (`desktop/build/icon.svg`) is the wheel in the accent colour (#a78bfa) on a circle in the dark theme's background (#0e0e12), filling the canvas and transparent outside the circle; the rounded tile of D-072 is gone. The sidebar and dialog logos (`helm-glyph*.svg`) are the bare wheel in currentColor, accent, white and ink. `desktop/build/tray.svg` (accent) and `trayTemplate.svg` (black, for the macOS menu bar) are the tray sources. `npm run icons` renders all of it with a headless Chrome, Edge or Brave: `icon.png`, `icon.ico`, `icon.icns` (now made by macOS's `iconutil`, so it carries the 2x sizes; elsewhere it is built by hand as before), the four tray images, and for the web client `hosted/web/public/apple-touch-icon.png`, beside `favicon.svg`, which `hosted/web/index.html` now links. Every output was checked for an alpha of 0 in the corners and the two colours.
Why: user request; the app icon showed as a white square in places and the wheel was the old outline design.
