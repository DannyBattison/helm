## 2026-11-03 · Accessible review controls

Use native checkbox inputs, preserve their existing visual treatment, and provide names from settings rows. Shared tabs support arrow keys; compound clickable rows support Enter and Space without activating nested controls. Dialogs name their contents, contain keyboard focus and restore it on close while allowing portalled selection menus. Focus uses an outline that survives inline shadows. Respect reduced-motion preferences and give statuses distinct icons as well as colors. Raise muted text contrast in both themes.
