## 2026-10-28 · Custom shortcuts, and Settings → Shortcuts as a folding group

You can make your own keyboard shortcuts under Settings → Shortcuts → Custom shortcuts (`settings.customShortcuts`). Two kinds for now: start a new session, which opens the new session dialog prefilled and never starts anything by itself, or go to a page or a repository (optionally on one of its tabs). The prefill covers everything the dialog has: repository (or free agent), base branch, task, agent and model, permissions, budget, extra repositories, other agents to ask, compare runs with their variants, and the folder of a free agent. Not covered: attachments and the "start after another session" choice, since both point at data that does not outlast a shortcut. A new session shortcut with no repository uses the repository of the page you are on. Custom shortcuts follow the same rules as the built-in ones (a modifier is needed, no duplicates across built-in and custom) and are listed in Search with their keys. Settings now has a "Shortcuts" group holding "Keyboard shortcuts" and "Custom shortcuts", and both it and "Accounts" fold away when clicked.
Why: user request. Prefill only, because starting an agent from a stray key press costs money.
