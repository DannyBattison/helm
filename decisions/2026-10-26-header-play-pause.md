## 2026-10-26 · Play / pause all lives in the header

Continue-all and Interrupt-all moved from the sidebar's Repositories label to the title bar, next to the inbox. They are always shown and disabled when there is nothing to act on, instead of appearing and disappearing, so the header does not shift. A tutorial bubble (`play-pause`) introduces them before the inbox bubble.

Also: the new session dialog follows the "Send messages with" setting (Enter, or Cmd/Ctrl+Enter); buttons render Enter as ↵; and the count in session filter pills is its own right-aligned, tabular-number partition.
