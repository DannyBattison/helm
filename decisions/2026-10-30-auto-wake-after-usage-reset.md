## 2026-10-30 · Auto-wake agents after a usage limit resets

Settings → Usage has "Wake agents after a reset" (off by default) and a choice of window, session (5-hour, the default) or week. When on, helm checks every minute for sessions paused at a plan limit it set, or that failed while a plan window was at 100%, and once the window they wait for has reset it tells them to continue. This works for any agent with plan windows (Claude Code and Codex). A session stopped by the weekly limit always waits for the week, since the 5-hour reset would not free it. It is off by default because it sends messages without anyone asking. The reset is only acted on after a fresh plan reading, or two minutes after the reset time, so a stale reading cannot wake agents into a limit that is still full.
